Student Tools Hub

Study tools for students: turn handwritten notes and course material into clean digital documents, and get help from an AI tutor that answers from your own material.

Open the app

  • Transcribe handwritten notes, PDFs and documents into Google Docs
  • Summarise, review and reference your material
  • Chat with an AI tutor and keep a personal Knowledge Bay

Sign-in with Google is optional and is used only to save your documents to your own Google Drive and to sync your data. For adults aged 18 and over.
